# Privacy Policy for Book Buddy

**App Name:** Book Buddy  
**Developer:** KUNDURU RANJITH KUMAR  
**Package ID:** com.bookbuddy.my_educational_app  
**Last Updated:** 5 September 2026

Book Buddy is a document reading, conversion, compression and study application developed by KUNDURU RANJITH KUMAR.

This Privacy Policy explains how Book Buddy handles information when you use the application.

## Information Collection

Book Buddy does not require users to create an account or sign in for its normal document and study features.

Book Buddy does not intentionally collect names, phone numbers, addresses, advertising identifiers or other personal profile information.

## Documents and Files

Book Buddy allows users to open, read, scan, convert, compress and organise PDF, DOCX and image files.

Supported document-processing operations are performed locally on the user's device.

Book Buddy uses Android's document access system so users choose which files the application may access.

Book Buddy does not upload users' documents to a developer-operated server as part of its normal document-processing features.

## Document Scanning and OCR

Book Buddy may use on-device document scanning and optical character recognition technologies to recognise text from scanned pages and images.

Recognised text may be stored locally to provide document search, indexing and study features.

## Study Features

Information such as reading progress, notes, bookmarks, flashcards, revision items, courses and study tasks is stored locally on the user's device.

## Advertising

Book Buddy does not display advertisements.

Book Buddy does not use the Google Advertising ID for advertising, behavioural tracking or user profiling.

## Analytics and Tracking

Book Buddy does not use advertising analytics or behavioural tracking services in the current version.

## Internet Access

Some third-party components included in Book Buddy may have network capability for supporting functionality.

Book Buddy does not use network access to sell document contents, personal information or study data to advertisers.

## Data Sharing

Book Buddy does not sell users' personal information.

Book Buddy does not sell document contents or locally stored study information to advertisers or other third parties.

## Data Storage and Deletion

Document history, reading progress, notes, flashcards, indexes and other study information may be stored locally on the device.

Users can remove locally stored information through Book Buddy where supported, clear the application's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the application.

## Children's Privacy

Book Buddy is a general document and study productivity application.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Book Buddy's features or data-handling practices change.

The latest version will always be made available through this privacy-policy page.
