OriginInk is a creator-focused platform for publishing, discovering and managing original artwork, manga, comics and related creative content. This Privacy Policy explains what information OriginInk collects, how it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account information

When you create or use an OriginInk account, we may process information such as:

- Email address
- Display name
- Username
- Authentication identifiers used to keep your account signed in securely
- Profile information you choose to provide

Authentication is provided using Firebase Authentication.

### Content you provide

When you use OriginInk, we may store content and information you choose to submit, including:

- Artwork, illustrations, manga and comic pages
- Titles, descriptions, genres and other work metadata
- Human or AI-assisted creation labels and related declarations
- Public/private visibility choices
- Competition entries
- Upvotes, follows and other community interactions
- Reports, report descriptions and moderation-related information

Publicly published works, creator names, usernames and related public profile information may be visible to other OriginInk users.

Private Art Vault content is not displayed publicly. It may still be processed by OriginInk's backend to provide storage, account management, safety, originality screening and other requested features.

## 2. Originality and Duplicate Screening

When a creator labels an upload as Human-created, OriginInk may perform originality screening before public publishing.

This screening may include:

- SHA-256 fingerprint comparison for exact duplicates
- Perceptual image fingerprint comparison for visually similar works already indexed by OriginInk
- Public-web image matching through Google Cloud Vision Web Detection when that service is available within OriginInk's configured usage limits

For a public-web check, a temporary copy or temporary signed URL of the selected image may be processed by Google Cloud Vision solely to obtain image-matching results for the requested originality check.

Possible matches are treated as review signals. OriginInk does not claim that automated matching alone proves plagiarism, copyright infringement or legal ownership.

If external matching is unavailable or the configured monthly usage limit has been reached, OriginInk may continue using internal duplicate and similarity screening.

## 3. Reports and Moderation

Users may report content for reasons such as suspected plagiarism or copyright concerns, misleading AI labels, spam, impersonation, harassment or inappropriate content.

Reports may include the selected reason and optional information supplied by the reporting user.

OriginInk administrators or approved moderators may review reported or held content when necessary to investigate a report, enforce platform rules, correct misleading labels, restrict content, restore content or take other appropriate moderation action.

Moderation actions and reasons may be recorded for safety, accountability and abuse prevention.

## 4. How We Use Information

OriginInk uses information to:

- Create and maintain user accounts
- Authenticate users securely
- Store and display public creative works
- Store private Art Vault content
- Provide manga chapter and series features
- Provide creator search, discovery, follows, upvotes, Trends and competitions
- Perform originality and duplicate screening
- Process user reports and moderation actions
- Prevent spam, abuse, fraud and misuse
- Maintain service security and reliability
- Diagnose technical problems and improve app functionality

OriginInk does not sell users' personal information or uploaded creative content.

## 5. Service Providers

OriginInk currently uses third-party infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service, including:

### Google Firebase

Firebase Authentication is used for account sign-in and authentication. Google may process technical information as necessary to provide Firebase services.

### Supabase

Supabase is used for backend database services, file storage, server-side functions and related application infrastructure. Account information, uploaded works, metadata and platform activity may be stored or processed through Supabase infrastructure.

### Google Cloud Vision

When enabled for Human-work originality screening, Google Cloud Vision Web Detection may process the selected image or a temporary signed image URL to search for possible public-web image matches.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices and only for the services OriginInk uses from them.

## 6. Public and Private Content

Content you choose to publish publicly may be visible to other users and may include your creator display name, username, work title, description, creation label, genre, ranking information and uploaded media.

Content marked Private is intended to remain available only through your account and authorised backend operations. Private content is not included in normal public discovery feeds.

You should only upload content that you have the right to upload and share.

## 7. Data Retention

OriginInk retains account information and uploaded content while your account or content remains active, or as necessary to operate the service.

Deleted or removed works may leave limited audit, safety, moderation, anti-abuse or provenance records where reasonably necessary to protect the platform, resolve reports or maintain service integrity.

Temporary files created specifically for originality screening are intended to be removed after the screening process completes.

Users who are minors should use the service with the involvement or permission of a parent or guardian where required by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.
